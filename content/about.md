## Hi, I'm Oleg.

I'm an AI Systems Architect and startup technical leader with 7+ years building production systems — from scrappy MVPs to infrastructure handling millions of requests.

My current focus is **agentic AI infrastructure**: designing the tools and patterns that let autonomous agents work reliably in production. Before that, I spent years deep in Go, Kubernetes, and cloud-native architecture, shipping products across fintech, SaaS, and developer tooling.

## What I Do

**Technical Leadership** — I've taken multiple startups from zero to one, handling architecture decisions, team building, and the inevitable firefighting that comes with early-stage products.

**Open Source** — I maintain several projects including [LocalWave](https://github.com/OlegHQ/localwave) (a local-first iOS music player), [agentpack](https://github.com/OlegHQ/agentpack) (universal agent packaging toolkit), [microtunnel](https://github.com/OlegHQ/microtunnel) (self-hosted HTTPS tunnels), and [psd_sdk](https://github.com/OlegHQ/psd_sdk) (Dart PSD file reader).

**Writing** — I write about systems design, AI engineering, and the messy reality of building software. You can find my posts on this site and across [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com), [DEV Community](https://dev.to), and other platforms.

## Tech Stack

My primary tools: **Go**, **Rust**, **TypeScript**, **Python**, **Swift**. On the infrastructure side: **Kubernetes**, **AWS**, **Terraform**, **Docker**. For AI work: **LLM orchestration**, **agent frameworks**, **RAG systems**, **real-time streaming**.
